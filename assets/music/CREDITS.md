# Music

Every song is our own: the notes of Duck Friends' old songs (written for the game), played on the GeneralUser GS
SoundFont by S. Christian Collins (https://schristiancollins.com/generaluser.php). Its license lets anyone use it, with no
restriction, in their own music, private or commercial.

The files were rendered outside the game with TinySoundFont and given the 32-bit games' "Medium" look (22 kHz, a little
grit, a small room). The notes and the scripts are kept in the project's files (music/new-sound-tests/songs-2026-11-16).
`lo/` holds the same songs at 32 kbps in one channel, for Data saving and Poor connection.
