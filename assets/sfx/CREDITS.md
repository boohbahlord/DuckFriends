# Where every sound comes from

All of them are real recordings, free to use in a game: CC0 1.0 (public domain) unless said otherwise.
Each was cut, darkened and made smaller by `tools/sfx/build.py` (the recordings themselves aren't in
the repo). The lobby countdown and the new round are played on the GeneralUser GS SoundFont's recorded
instruments (its license: LICENSES.md).
