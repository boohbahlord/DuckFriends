# Who is the Quack? The plan

Oct 25, 2026 · owner

## The game

Every duck's Secret Card shows the same secret word except one, the Quack, whose card doesn't. Players take turns giving a clue over voice. After everyone has given one, the room walks to a side: another lap of clues, or Vote Out the Quack. Voting out is done by walking onto a duck's tile. A voted-out innocent goes grey and sits out, and the clues go on, until the Quack is caught or only two ducks are left.

In the lobby's mode picker it shows as **Who is the Quack?**, with the tip: "Everyone's Secret Card has the same word except the Quack's. Give clues, then walk onto the duck you think is faking it." It needs at least 3 players (computers count in TEST rooms).

## A round, step by step

Updated with your answers of 25 October. A Round is one secret word. Rounds keep coming, each with a new word, a new word picker and a new Quack, until a back-to-lobby vote passes, as in the other modes.

1. **The word.** Before the board loads, one random player picks the secret word in secret, plus a short category such as "Breakfast". Everyone else sees "{Name} is picking the secret word…". A "Need an idea?" button fills in a word from a built-in list. The picker is never the Quack, and the job goes round so everyone gets a turn.
2. **The Secret Cards.** Each player gets a face-down Secret Card on their own screen. Hold it (or hold a finger on it) to peek, so nobody sharing their screen gives it away. Innocent ducks see the word ("Pancake") and the category. The Quack's card shows only the category and "You are the Quack".
3. **Clue turns.** The duck whose turn it is sits on the cloud and their tile lights up. They say their clue over voice. For the first 12 seconds nobody can end the turn. After that the speaker ends it with Done (Enter). If they don't, every other player has to press Skip to move on. There is no timer. The Quack is never first.
4. **Another lap, or vote?** Once everyone still in has given a clue, the board turns into two sides: **Vote Out the Quack** on the left, **Another round of clues** on the right. Everyone walks to a side and presses Vote. Another round starts the clue turns again as usual. A tie goes to the vote.
5. **Vote Out the Quack.** The board shows the players' tiles again. Everyone walks onto the tile of the duck they suspect and presses Vote. There is no timer by default, so people can argue it out. A tie gets one quick revote with only the tied tiles; if that ties too, nobody is out and the clues go on.
6. **Voted out.** The cloud says "The room voted out {Name}…", a short drumroll, then:
   - **The Quack:** "…the Quack!" The room wins.
   - **An innocent duck:** "…not the Quack." Their tile and their duck go grey until the round ends. They can't give clues or vote, but can still walk and talk. The clue turns go on.
7. **The Quack wins** when only one other duck is left in. That last duck plays dead (the silly commands' dead look) and can't move for a few seconds while they and their tile turn grey. The Quack's Secret Card flips over in front of everyone, showing the category and "You are the Quack".
8. **Talk it over.** Everyone sees the word, the Quack and how each duck voted. End turn moves on to the stats, then the next round.

## The board and the look

- **The board** is one tile per player, in the color (and pattern) of that player's card, see-through over the map like every other board. Each tile shows the player's duck and name. The duck on the tile changes the moment its player changes anything in Customize mid-game. Tiles sit in a grid that fits the player count, on a phone in portrait as well as a computer.
- **During clues** the speaker's tile is lit up and their duck is on the cloud.
- **The two sides** (Vote Out the Quack on the left, Another round of clues on the right) take over the board between laps, like the Would You Rather tiles.
- **During the vote** your own tile is dimmed: you can walk over it but not vote for yourself. Grey tiles (voted-out ducks) can't be voted for.
- **Voted out:** the tile and the duck on it turn greyscale, and so does that player's duck on the board, until the round ends.
- **The reveal:** the Quack's tile flashes. Moment specials play as they do now: the winners' specials, and the Loser's X on a caught Quack. When the Quack wins, the last duck plays dead and greys out, and the Quack's card flips over for everyone.
- **The Secret Card** is a small playing-card shape with the multicolored title letters, bottom left above the bar, out of the board's way.
- **The mode's symbol:** three small yellow ducks with one dark duck and a "?" over it, in the same flat style as the other symbols.
- **Sounds:** the existing boops and letters for the cloud, plus a short drumroll before the reveal, made the same synthesized way as today's sounds.

## Lobby settings

These show in the lobby's settings only when Who is the Quack? is picked. The defaults are in bold.

| Setting | Choices |
| --- | --- |
| Who picks the word | **A random player**, or the built-in list |
| Time to pick the word | 30 s, **60 s**, 90 s (when time runs out, a built-in word is used) |
| Quacks | **1**, or 2 (only with 6 or more players) |
| Vote timer | **Off**, 30 s, 60 s, 90 s |

The usual settings (map, free roam, low gravity, silly commands) work as they do now.

## Small choices I'll make unless you say otherwise

- My reading of the 12 seconds: nobody, the speaker included, can end a clue turn in its first 12 seconds. Tell me if you meant only the others.
- Clue order is random each lap, never the Quack first. Voted-out ducks are skipped.
- Both the two-sided choice and the vote out need every duck still in, the same as a bracket match, unless the host sets a vote timer.
- The word picker gives clues and votes like anyone else; they just already know the word, as every innocent does.
- A player who joins mid-round walks around and chats, and plays from the next round (no Secret Card mid-round).
- If the Quack leaves mid-round, the round ends with "The Quack fled!" and moves on to a new word. If the host drops mid-round and someone else takes over, the round starts over with a new word (the new host can't open the old cards).
- There's no limit on clue laps; the room decides when to vote.
- Computers in TEST rooms pick words from the built-in list, "give" a clue in a chat bubble (they can't talk), and vote for a random duck, leaning a little toward the real Quack.
- Nothing in the player menu or chat hints at who the Quack is until the reveal.

## How it gets built and tested

- The mode's rules go in their own file (like brackets have theirs), so it stays clear of the other threads working on The Court and the skip-round vote.
- One thing online needs care: the room's state is shared with every browser, so the secret word can't sit in it in plain sight, or anyone opening the browser's tools could read it. Each player's card is locked so only their own browser can open it. The host's browser runs the game, so it knows the answer, the same way it already sees everything. Nothing needs changing in Firebase.
- I'll test every map, phone in portrait and computer, in TEST rooms with computers and online with several browsers, and send screenshots.
- The pull request waits for the batch merge with the others; nothing goes live until then.
